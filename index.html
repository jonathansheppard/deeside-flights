Before you travel

This page is a quick guide and can be up to 15 minutes behind. For gates, security times and the final word on your flight, check Manchester Airport's own departures board and your airline's app.

If your flight is cancelled or badly delayed, your airline has to offer you help. Check what you are entitled to on the Civil Aviation Authority's website.
